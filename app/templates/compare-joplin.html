Deeperguard vs Joplin

Joplin is a flexible open-source notebook with optional cloud sync and rich plugins. Deeperguard is a hosted zero-knowledge vault with OCR in the browser. Joplin’s desktop OCR runs offline; encryption is off until you enable it.

Where Joplin wins

  • Plugins: Extend editors, sync, and workflows.
  • Sync choice: Use your own cloud or self-host.
  • Storage: Bring your own storage — you pay your sync provider.

Where Deeperguard wins

  • Encryption by default: No optional E2EE step.
  • Browser + mobile OCR: Scan and search without a desktop install.
  • Zero setup: Create a vault and write — check it yourself.
Can Deeperguard or its host read my notes?

Stored notes: no, except text you choose to send to Ask AI with Ollama Cloud, which passes through our server to ollama.com without being stored.
