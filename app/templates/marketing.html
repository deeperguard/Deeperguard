Open notes

Joplin

Popular open-source notebook with Markdown, plugins, and bring-your-own sync (Dropbox, WebDAV, Joplin Cloud, etc.).

  • Open source & self-host friendly: Large community, many sync targets, rich Markdown tooling.

Privacy by architecture

Privacy by architecture, not by policy

A database copy is still ciphertext. Reading a note requires the vault password on a device you unlock. The details of how that key is made are in How encryption works.

🔐

Local Key Derivation

Your password is processed with Argon2id directly on your device. The server receives an SRP verification proof and only stores unreadable encrypted chunks, except text you choose to send to Ask AI with Ollama Cloud, which passes through our server to ollama.com without being stored.

⚡

Fast Offline PWA

Open the app in Safari, Chrome, or Firefox. Add it to your home screen for an app-like experience with offline access, offline caching, and instant launch.

🔄

Encrypted Sync Engine

Write notes on your iPhone, continue on your laptop. Multi-device sync happens smoothly in the background while keeping everything end-to-end encrypted.

📄

Private Client OCR

Photograph receipts, invoices, and documents. Client-side WebAssembly OCR parses text in your browser so you can search inside PDFs without leaking plaintext.

🔑

Passkeys & TOTP 2FA

Use Face ID, Touch ID, or hardware security keys to sign into your account safely. Protect sensitive individual notes with edit locks while screen sharing.

💾

Resilient Backups

Sleep soundly with daily encrypted email snapshots or automated backups pushed directly to your personal pCloud drive—always encrypted with your keys.

How encryption works

How Deeperguard works

Create an account, unlock the vault on this device, then write and sync. The cryptography stays on the device. You can verify zero-knowledge yourself in DevTools — SRP proofs on login, ciphertext-only sync (except Ask AI) — or open View server copy on any item in the app.

Create your free account

Sign up with your email and a strong master password. You start on the Free plan — upgrade to Basic or Pro for higher limits and Pro-only features.

Unlock your local vault

This device derives the vault key locally with Argon2id. Sign-in uses SRP-6a, so the server stores a verifier, not the password. Sync stores AES-GCM 256 ciphertext.

Write, scan & sync

Compose notes, organize with tags, attach files, scan documents, and search instantly. Everything remains encrypted before transmission.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can Deeperguard or its host read my notes?

Stored notes: no. Notes, titles, and attachments are encrypted in your browser using AES-GCM before syncing. We only store encrypted binary ciphertext, except text you choose to send to Ask AI with Ollama Cloud, which passes through our server to ollama.com without being stored. Your vault password is never transmitted in readable form.

Can I review the source code?

Yes. The client source is available for review on GitHub so you can inspect encryption, sync, and client logic. The license allows inspection and audit; it does not allow using the software in production or commercially without a separate agreement. See LICENSE on GitHub. You can also verify behavior in the browser on /verify.

Do I have to download or install an application from an app store?

No. Deeperguard runs as a fast Progressive Web App (PWA) in any modern browser. On iOS Safari or Android Chrome, simply select "Add to Home Screen" for a full-screen, offline-capable experience.

How does document search work if notes are zero-knowledge?

Document OCR runs directly on your device using WebAssembly (Tesseract.js). The extracted text is encrypted client-side and attached to your note's ciphertext before syncing, allowing full-text search without compromising privacy.

What are the plans and pricing?

New accounts start on Free ({{ free.storage_quota_mb }} MB, always €0, {{ free.active_sessions }} signed-in devices) with sync, attachments, import/export, and on-device OCR — not passkeys, account 2FA, email or pCloud backups, reminders, or the vault authenticator. Basic is €{{ "%.2f"|format(basic.suggested_price_eur_month) }}/mo or €{{ "%.0f"|format(basic.suggested_price_eur_year) }}/yr ({{ basic.storage_quota_mb }} MB, {{ basic.active_sessions }} devices) with the same feature set as Free. Pro is €{{ "%.2f"|format(pro.suggested_price_eur_month) }}/mo or €{{ "%.0f"|format(pro.suggested_price_eur_year) }}/yr ({{ (pro.storage_quota_mb / 1024)|int }} GB, unlimited devices) and adds passkeys, account 2FA, daily email backup, pCloud backup, reminders, and the vault authenticator. Existing accounts keep their stored plan.

How many devices can I stay signed in on?

Free allows {{ free.active_sessions }} signed-in devices at once, Basic allows {{ basic.active_sessions }}, and Pro is unlimited. If you hit the limit, sign out one device from the sign-in screen, then sign in again. Signing out ends that device’s session; your notes are not deleted.

What happens if I lose my password?

Because Deeperguard is zero-knowledge, we cannot reset or recover your master vault password. Without it, ciphertext on our servers cannot be decrypted — there is no “forgot password” email that restores access.

Set up protection early: On Pro, turn on daily encrypted email backups and/or pCloud encrypted backups in Settings as soon as your vault is ready. Archives stay encrypted; you still need the vault password to open them, but you will not lose the only copy if a device fails.

If sign-in credentials drift but you still know your vault password, the app can guide you through vault login recovery using encrypted samples on the server — that is not a master-password reset.

Can I import my existing notes from Standard Notes?

Yes. Deeperguard provides built-in Standard Notes backup import. You can upload your decrypted or encrypted Standard Notes export file directly in Settings.
