Where each product wins

Where Notesnook wins

  • Free plan: Mature free tier for everyday encrypted notes.
  • Native apps: Polished desktop and mobile clients as the default workflow.
  • User base: Established privacy-notes community and sync service.

Where Deeperguard wins

  • In-browser OCR: Search scans and PDFs without installing a desktop app.
  • Vault 2FA codes: Keep authenticator secrets in the same encrypted vault.

FAQ

Common questions

Can Deeperguard or its host read my notes?

Stored notes: no. Notes, titles, and attachments are encrypted in your browser before sync. The service stores ciphertext only, except text you choose to send to Ask AI with Ollama Cloud, which passes through our server to ollama.com without being stored.
