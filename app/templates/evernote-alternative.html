What you keep

Search, web access, offline

  • Search inside scans — on-device OCR indexes text from photos and PDFs before encryption.
  • Web access — use Deeperguard in the browser; add it to your home screen as a PWA.
  • Offline — read and write in your local vault without a connection.
What’s different

Encryption, EU server, Ask AI

  • Zero-knowledge by design — AES-GCM on your device; sync moves ciphertext (except Ask AI), not readable notes.
  • Server in Vienna, Austria (EU) — operated by Schutten Invest (Netherlands) on netcup hosting.
  • Ask AI exception — only when you send text to Ask AI with Ollama Cloud; it passes through our server to ollama.com without being stored.
