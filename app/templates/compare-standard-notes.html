Migration

Move from Standard Notes in three steps

1. Export from Standard Notes

Create a Standard Notes backup export (encrypted or decrypted) from your existing account.

2. Create your vault

Register at deeperguard.com/register and unlock your vault on this device.

3. Import in Settings

Open Settings → Backup & import and choose Import Standard Notes or Deeperguard backup. Encrypted backups and decrypted exports (.zip or .txt) are supported.

FAQ

Questions Standard Notes shoppers ask

Can Deeperguard or its host read my notes?

No. Notes, titles, and attachments are encrypted in your browser before sync. The service stores ciphertext only. Your vault password is never sent in readable form.

Is Deeperguard more secure than Standard Notes?

No — same privacy class (zero-knowledge E2EE), different product surface. Standard Notes has a longer track record; Deeperguard adds a PWA, on-device OCR, and SN import.

What if I lose my vault password?

There is no password reset. Enable daily encrypted email backups and/or pCloud encrypted backups in Settings as soon as your vault is ready — archives stay encrypted, but you keep a copy if a device fails.
