Trademarks
----------
CC0 covers the copyright in the drawings only. The logos and names are
trademarks of their respective owners. They are shown only to identify the
account the user added (nominative use), never to suggest endorsement or
partnership, and never in our own marketing material or screenshots. On a
removal request from a trademark owner the slug is dropped from the
allow-list and the account falls back to the letter avatar.
