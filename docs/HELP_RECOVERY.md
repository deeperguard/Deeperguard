Deeperguard support will never ask for your password, recovery codes, or 2FA codes. No one outside the app can restore your account — only the recovery steps shown here.

## Two locks, two ways back

Deeperguard has two locks. Signing in proves the account is yours. Unlocking the vault opens your notes on your device, and only your master password does that. Each lock has its own way back, and they are not interchangeable. Find both in Settings → "If something goes wrong".

### Recovery codes (account sign-in)

Recovery codes are for signing in to your account if you lose your passkeys. If you lose access to a device or passkey, use the recovery codes you saved when you created them in Settings. Each code works once, after the waiting period you chose (at least 72 hours). Recovery codes do not open your vault.

### Vault recovery key

The vault recovery key is for opening your vault if you forget your master password. Create it in Settings and store it somewhere safe, away from your device. It does not sign you in to your account. Deeperguard cannot read it or show it again. Deeperguard cannot reset your vault password or decrypt your notes for you.

If you lose your 2FA authenticator app, Deeperguard has no codes that replace it, so keep a backup of that app.

## What we will never do

We will never ask you to paste your master password, recovery codes, or 2FA codes into email, chat, or a website that isn’t this app.

## Restore from backup

Encrypted backups restore only with your account password in Settings → Import backup. It is the "Restore Deeperguard backup" button in the Restore & import card.
